PRIVACY POLICY

Your sermons remain yours.

Effective August 25, 2026

Overview

Sermon Desk is designed to keep your sermon preparation private. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Sermon data

Your sermons, outlines, slides, preferences, and related content are stored locally on your Mac. Sermon Desk does not upload that content to the developer. Backup, import, print, and export actions use files and locations that you select.

Online Scripture lookup

When you choose to look up a Bible passage, Sermon Desk sends the Scripture reference you enter and the translation you select to Bible API so the passage can be returned. Sermon Desk does not send your manuscript or sermon library with that request. Bible API may process technical information, such as an IP address, under its own practices.

Data collection and tracking

Sermon Desk does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data. It does not sell data and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data control

You control the sermon files and backups you create. You may delete sermons within the app and remove local app data through macOS. Files exported to locations you choose remain under your control.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Sermon Desk’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email rocpastor411@gmail.com. Do not include private sermon content.
