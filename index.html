SUPPORT

Common questions

Where are my sermons saved?

Sermon Desk stores your sermon library locally on your Mac. Use Backup regularly to save a portable copy in a location you choose.

How do I create slides?

Select text in the manuscript and choose To Slides. Open Slides to organize the presentation and export it to PowerPoint.

How do I build an outline?

Select the manuscript text you want and choose To Outline. Open Outline to arrange and print the listener handout.

How does Preach mode work?

Choose Preach to open a focused reading view with large text, simple navigation, a timer, and a reading guide.

How do I restore a backup?

Choose Import, select a Sermon Desk backup, and review the confirmation when the import is complete.

How do I change writing defaults?

Open Appearance to choose the theme and set default font, size, text color, and highlight for new writing.
